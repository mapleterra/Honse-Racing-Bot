# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

\---

## 1\. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Horse Racing Bot ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is stored. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

\---

## 2\. What Data We Collect

The Bot collects and temporarily stores only the minimum data required to provide its features:

|Data|Why it is collected|How long it is kept|
|-|-|-|
|Discord User ID|To track race result subscriptions so you can be pinged when a race finishes|Until the race ends or you unsubscribe — deleted automatically|
|Discord Message ID|To track subscription card messages so they can be deleted after a race|Until the race ends — deleted automatically|
|Discord Channel ID|To know which channel to post results and delete subscription messages in|Until the race ends — deleted automatically|

**We do not collect:**

* Your username or display name
* Your email address
* Your Discord messages or chat history
* Any personal information beyond your Discord User ID

\---

## 3\. How Data Is Used

Your Discord User ID is used solely to:

* Mention you in the race results channel when a race you subscribed to finishes
* Allow you to manage your own subscriptions via `/subscribe`, `/unsubscribe`, and `/mysubscriptions`

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

\---

## 4\. Data Storage

All data is stored **in memory only** while the Bot is running. This means:

* No data is written to a database or file on disk
* All subscription data is automatically cleared when the Bot restarts
* Subscription data for a specific race is deleted the moment that race's result is posted

\---

## 5\. Third-Party Services

The Bot fetches publicly available data from the following third-party websites:

* **Equibase** (equibase.com) — US race cards and results
* **Racing Post** (racingpost.com) — UK/IRE/international race cards and results
* **OddsChecker** (oddschecker.com) — Multi-bookmaker odds
* **Google News RSS** — Horse racing news articles

When the Bot retrieves data from these services, your Discord User ID is not shared with them. These requests are made by the Bot's server, not by your device. Please refer to each service's own privacy policy for information on how they handle data.

\---

## 6\. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for any purpose.

\---

## 7\. Your Rights

You have the right to:

* **View** your subscriptions at any time using `/mysubscriptions`
* **Delete** your subscription data at any time using `/unsubscribe` or by removing your 🔔 reaction
* **Request removal** of any data we hold by contacting us directly

Since all data is in-memory only and tied to active subscriptions, removing your subscriptions removes all data we hold about you.

\---

## 8\. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the Bot, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.
